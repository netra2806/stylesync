Neural Computer Vision & Precomputed Vectors

How StyleSync Decodes Fashion

Unlike conventional keyword search that relies on subjective product tags, StyleSync understands fashion the way human eyes do — through patterns, color harmonies, silhouettes, and fabric textures.

01

Visual Ingestion & Preprocessing

When you upload an outfit inspiration image (from Pinterest, Instagram, or street photography), our system verifies resolution, sanitizes inputs, and formats the image tensor through standard Vision Transformer bicubic resizing and RGB normalization.

02

CLIP ViT Feature Vectorization

The processed tensor is fed into the deep neural layers of OpenAI's CLIP (Contrastive Language-Image Pretraining) vision encoder. The model extracts a high-density 512-dimensional numerical embedding vector that encapsulates visual semantics: colors, drapery, collars, cuts, and material sheen.

03

Precomputed Database Matching

Every product in the StyleSync database is vectorized ahead of time upon insertion. Because embeddings are stored in SQLite and cached, the query requires zero re-training or full catalog re-encoding, resulting in lightning-fast millisecond matching.

04

Cosine Similarity & Nearest-Neighbour Ranking

Using vector geometry, we calculate the cosine of the angle between your query vector and all product vectors: similarity = (A • B) / (||A|| * ||B||). The closest 10 visual matches are surfaced instantly with similarity scores and direct boutique links.
